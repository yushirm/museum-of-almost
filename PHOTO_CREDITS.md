# Photograph sources and rights

The Dreaming Wing uses three locally stored source photographs. They were resized, converted to WebP and stripped of embedded metadata before being added to the repository. The live Museum does not request images from third-party servers.

These photographs retain their source rights status and are not covered by the Museum's all-rights-reserved claim.
