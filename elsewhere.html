COLLECTIONS STORE / CATALOGUE 0

Objects with provenance problems.

Twelve records are shelved below. Nothing is selected.

CATALOGUE RULE 0: An object may be stored here only if the record describing it is more confident than the world that supposedly produced it. DOCUMENTATION RULE 0: Condition reports may certify material, inscription, damage, location and observed change; impossible provenance remains transcription, not observation. If object and record disagree, preserve the discrepancy rather than inventing an explanation.

PACKING BENCH / CRATE TRIAL 0

One crate. Three incompatible jobs.

The provenance can remain impossible while the packing problem stays stubbornly physical. Choose an accession and the bench changes what it supports, restrains and refuses to assume.

PACKING RULE 0: Start with material, condition, dimensions and handling risk. A crate can protect an object from shock, abrasion and environmental hazards; it cannot make the catalogue story true.

SELECT ACCESSION FOR THE CRATE TRIAL

C0.002 / FLAT SUPPORT BEFORE DESTINATION

Observed problem: a small paper object with two punched marks. The cancelled railway is a record problem; bends, abrasion and pressure are material ones.

Bench decision: keep it flat between stable supports, restrain movement without loading the punched areas, and carry the accession identity beside the object rather than writing new certainty onto it.

FIT: PAPER SUPPORT · PROVENANCE: UNCHANGED

C0.006 / BRACE THE SIGN, NOT THE STORY

Observed problem: enamelled architectural hardware with permanent anchor scars. Its authority over a missing exit does not tell handlers which edge can safely carry load.

Bench decision: support the stable body, isolate projecting hardware from impact, and record orientation before movement so a handling change cannot masquerade as an old condition feature.

FIT: RIGID BRACING · PROVENANCE: UNCHANGED

C0.010 / THE BOX IS THE HANDLING UNIT

Observed problem: a closed archival box with a fixed dark shape that does not reproduce. Staff can verify the enclosure and its condition without claiming custody of a literal shadow.

Bench decision: immobilise the enclosure, preserve orientation, document the exterior before and after movement, and leave the impossible optical claim exactly where it belongs: in the record.

FIT: ENCLOSURE SUPPORT · PROVENANCE: UNCHANGED

SHIFT HANDOVER / INCIDENT 0

One building failure. Four ordinary consequences.

CALL 01 · AIR HANDLING OFFLINE. Freeze non-essential movement in the paper/film and optical zones. Stable conditions become the problem before any accession's impossible story does.

CALL 02 · WATER ROUTE SUSPECT. Check Zone A first and keep the service route clear. Material vulnerability sets the priority; a ticket for an unbuilt railway is still made of paper.

CALL 03 · IMAGING BENCH DARK. Suspend comparison work on C0.008–C0.010 and record the documentation gap. Missing images are not evidence that the objects changed while nobody was looking.

HANDOVER · FREIGHT LIFT UNAVAILABLE. Stabilise in place until the ordinary handling route returns. The store may know where an accession is while temporarily having no safe way to move it.

SHIFT RULE 0: Facilities changes what staff can safely do. It does not rewrite where an impossible object supposedly came from.

LOST & FOUND / ELSEWHERE

Nothing here is evidence.

Catalogue 0 is a fictional museum department. Its objects, accession records, contradictions and provenance are invented for this space. They are not claims about real history, real institutions, or real people.

The collection does not accept visitor submissions, names, stories, uploads or free text. Nothing you open here is stored. Reloading returns every drawer to its ordinary closed state.
