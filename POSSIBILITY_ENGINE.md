# The Possibility Engine / Success Archives

This record documents the fifth instrument in **Deep Space / Almost**.

## Pre-code concept gate

### Concept A — The Evidence Ladder

The conventional next step was to extend each Unsolved Room question with an observation → constraint → possible-progress ladder. It would have been clear and scientifically disciplined, but it mostly repeated the gallery's existing card/readout grammar.

### Concept B — The Falsification Chamber

Borrow a mechanic from laboratory test rigs and fault-isolation engineering. A visitor applies fixed pieces of evidence to a small possibility map and watches narrower claims remain open, come under pressure, or stop surviving the case.

### Concept C — The Museum Keeps Only Successful Failures

Break the usual newest-first archive convention. Keep an archive of moments when evidence forced a useful revision: a deficit that exposed a deeper particle property, an expected slowdown that became acceleration, or a long-standing prediction that crossed into direct detection.

**Concept A was discarded.** It was useful but least additive to the existing Deep Space interaction language. Concepts B and C were merged into **The Possibility Engine / Success Archives**.

## What “success” means

The archive does not celebrate being wrong for its own sake and does not claim that one experiment permanently closes a scientific field. A success record is a case where evidence materially changed the viable explanatory map.

The interface therefore uses only four categorical states:

- **Open** — the displayed evidence has not yet materially narrowed the possibility.
- **Pressured** — the displayed evidence makes the possibility harder to maintain, without presenting it as eliminated.
- **Retired by this evidence** — the specifically worded claim no longer survives the evidence represented by the case.
- **Survived this evidence** — the possibility remains consistent with the evidence shown; this is not a probability or a declaration of final truth.

The bar lengths are visual categories only. They are not confidence intervals, posterior probabilities, model weights, or significance values.

## Archive records

### Deficit → transformation — solar neutrinos

For decades, solar-neutrino experiments detected fewer electron neutrinos than theoretical expectations. The later atmospheric and solar-neutrino results showed that neutrinos change flavour in flight. Neutrino oscillation requires non-zero neutrino mass, so a massless-neutrino description could not remain complete.

Source record: Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences, Nobel Prize in Physics 2015 press release and public background.

- https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/2015/press-release/
- https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/2015/popular-information/

### Expected slowing → acceleration — distant supernovae

Two teams used distant Type Ia supernovae to probe the history of cosmic expansion. They expected evidence of deceleration and instead found that the expansion is accelerating. The archive deliberately stops there: the observation constrains the expansion history, while the physical nature of dark energy remains unresolved.

Source record: Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences, Nobel Prize in Physics 2011 press release and public background.

- https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/2011/press-release/
- https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/2011/popular-information/

### Prediction → detection — gravitational waves

LIGO's two detectors observed the first direct gravitational-wave signal in September 2015. The observed waveform was consistent with a binary-black-hole merger predicted by general relativity, and the noise-only explanation for that event did not survive the reported statistical and waveform tests.

Source record: Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences, Nobel Prize in Physics 2017 press release and public background.

- https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/2017/press-release/
- https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/2017/popular-information/

## Runtime and privacy contract

- No new runtime request or remote asset.
- No visitor free-text, account, location, analytics, telemetry or tracking.
- No `localStorage`, `sessionStorage`, IndexedDB, cookie, URL-state, or click-history persistence.
- No polling, timer loop, animation loop, or scientific feed.
- The three cases and evidence steps are fixed local reference material.
- Switching cases resets the chamber; reload also resets it.
- The static archive remains visible without JavaScript.
- The new local HTML, CSS and JavaScript are part of the same-origin offline shell.
