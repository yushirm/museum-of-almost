- **Growth introduced:** **WEBLOG FOLIO TABS // THE TITLE STAYS UNTIL THE THOUGHT ENDS** makes each existing weblog header act like an index-card divider on viewports wider than 620 CSS pixels. While its own article is passing through the reading viewport, that post’s date and title remain briefly pinned near the top; when the article ends, the header leaves with it and the next post takes over. No scroll position is measured or stored. Narrow layouts remain ordinary flow, `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` restores static headers, and print also restores static headers.

The responsive and reduced-motion fallbacks also establish a boundary for future scroll-native experiments.
