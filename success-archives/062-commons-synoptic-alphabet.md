# 062 — COMMONS / NOW — The Synoptic Alphabet

Feature:

**The Synoptic Alphabet / Thirteen Discrete Stations**

Ancestor:

The fixed thirteen-point weather latch retained from COMMONS / NOW, especially the Weather Score's honest missing-value handling and the Solar Boundary Atlas's refusal to mistake a representation for direct observation.

Selection pressure:

Recent generations had become rich in derived verdicts, controls, scores and continuous-looking representations. The room needed a way to expose the current weather sample without another selector, ranking, sonification, world surface or inferred condition between sparse points.

Retained trait:

A shared, non-personal planetary snapshot with fixed sampling points, explicit acquisition bounds, textual authority and missing values preserved as missing.

Mutation introduced:

Thirteen non-interactive Museum station glyphs compress only the three weather fields already retained by the latch: temperature, wind speed and precipitation. Temperature and wind use fixed acquisition ranges; precipitation distinguishes zero, positive and missing. Numeric text remains authoritative. The instrument explicitly refuses wind direction, pressure, cloud cover, fronts, interpolation and inferred conditions because those variables were not measured here.

Crossover:

Borrowed the compression principle of synoptic meteorological station notation while deliberately avoiding standard WMO symbol semantics. This is a local Museum alphabet, not a standards-conformant station model.

Recent pattern intentionally avoided:

Not another audio encoding, solar/day-night atlas, continuous map, button-and-verdict instrument, ranking, completeness score or interpolation layer.

Resulting fitness:

- makes sparse observational structure legible at a glance without inventing weather between stations;
- introduces no new public-data request, polling, storage, visitor input, tracking, remote asset or dependency;
- preserves keyboard and screen-reader access through authoritative text while decorative glyph geometry remains `aria-hidden`;
- adds no motion and remains responsive, reduced-motion safe and static/offline compatible;
- repairs an inherited offline-shell omission that left the Solar Boundary Atlas assets outside precache;
- fixes the older Parallax Survey regression so a historical generation remains preserved without claiming permanent ownership of the current cache.
