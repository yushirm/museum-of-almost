- **Selection pressure:** Fourteen strong instruments had accumulated into a long scientific gallery, but most still behaved as self-contained islands. The highest-value growth was to increase relationships among them without adding another top-level slab.

- **Net visitor-facing surface change:** Slightly up when opened; effectively unchanged in the default scroll because the concordance is collapsed natively.
- **Pattern intentionally avoided:** Another control/readout instrument, generic table of contents, false confidence scoring, or decorative mystery.
- **Future evolutionary headroom created:** New instruments can join an existing evidence grammar or create new crosscuts. Later generations can evolve relationships among instruments as readily as they evolve individual instruments.
- **Resulting fitness:** Scientific literacy and gallery coherence increase while top-level section count, network footprint, persistence, custom interaction state and default visual burden stay unchanged.
