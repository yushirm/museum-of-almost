# Privacy contract

The Museum of Almost is intentionally private by design and local-first at runtime.

## Runtime behaviour

- No accounts, profiles, authentication or text entry.
- No analytics, telemetry, advertising, tracking pixels or behavioural logging.
- No external APIs, hosted fonts, third-party scripts or remote media.
- No cookies.
- No collection of names, locations, device identifiers or other personal information.
- The pocket catalogue stores only generated fictional fragment labels, progress counters and a random seed in the browser's local storage.
- **Save postcard** creates an image locally. It leaves the device only when the visitor deliberately moves or shares it.
- Optional ambient sound is synthesised in the browser and is silent until explicitly enabled.
- The service worker handles same-origin static files only so the museum can work offline.

## Repository rule

Do not add personal information about any person to this repository—not in source code, examples, fixtures, screenshots, issues, pull requests, documentation, commit messages or generated artifacts.

Do not add secrets, credentials, private URLs, private correspondence, real addresses, real phone numbers or identifying sample data. Use fictional and generic material only.

Do not enable a public deployment without a separate, explicit owner decision.
