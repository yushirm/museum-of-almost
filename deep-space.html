THE SECOND GALLERY · SPACE · TIME · THE UNKNOWN

Nothing here is close.

Deep space is not a backdrop. It is a laboratory for impossible scales: light that takes years to arrive, gravity strong enough to reshape time, invisible matter that outweighs the visible, and questions our best theories still cannot close.

No live feed. No account. No location. Every instrument on this page runs locally from fixed physical constants and transparent approximations.

A decorative local diagram, not a map. Distance is the first thing the universe refuses to draw to scale.

SUCCESS ARCHIVES · FILED BY WHAT CHANGED

Wrong turns worth keeping.

This archive refuses the usual newest-first timeline. It files cases by the kind of revision evidence forced: a deficit becoming transformation, expected slowing becoming acceleration, and a prediction becoming direct detection. “Success” here means a claim became more constrained, not that inquiry ended.

DEFICIT → TRANSFORMATION

Solar neutrinos

A missing-particle puzzle exposed a deeper assumption: neutrinos can change flavour, so the massless-neutrino picture could not remain complete.

Historical source record · Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences · Physics 2015

EXPECTED SLOWING → ACCELERATION

Accelerating universe

Distant supernovae did not preserve the expected cosmic slowdown. The surprise established accelerating expansion while leaving its deeper cause unresolved.

Historical source record · Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences · Physics 2011

PREDICTION → DETECTION

Gravitational waves

A century-old prediction crossed into direct observation when LIGO detected a signal consistent with a binary black-hole merger.

Historical source record · Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences · Physics 2017
