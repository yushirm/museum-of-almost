THE SECOND GALLERY · SPACE · TIME · THE UNKNOWN

Nothing here is close.

Deep space is not a backdrop. It is a laboratory for impossible scales: light that takes years to arrive, gravity strong enough to reshape time, invisible matter that outweighs the visible, and questions our best theories still cannot close.

No live feed. No account. No location. Every instrument on this page runs locally from fixed physical constants and transparent approximations.

A decorative local diagram, not a map. Distance is the first thing the universe refuses to draw to scale.

INSTRUMENT 01 · LIGHT AS A CLOCK

To look outward is to look backward.

Light is fast enough to define the metre, but the universe is much larger than “fast” is useful. Choose a scale and the instrument converts distance into one-way light travel time.

INSTRUMENT 03 · COSMIC INVENTORY

Most of the universe is named after what we do not know.

A rounded reference cosmology: only a small fraction is ordinary atomic matter. The larger shares are inferred from how the universe gravitates and expands.

4.9%

Percentages are rounded reference values from the standard cosmological model, not a live measurement or claim of final certainty.

INSTRUMENT 04 · THE UNSOLVED ROOM

The frontier is not empty. It is unfinished.

Choose an open question. Each card separates what observations already force us to take seriously from what remains genuinely unresolved.

What is dark matter?

What we know

Galaxies and clusters behave as though much more gravitating matter exists than we can see. Gravitational lensing maps the same hidden mass.

What remains open

We still do not know the underlying particle or field, or whether the final explanation requires a deeper change to gravity.

We can measure the edge of understanding without pretending we have crossed it.

Deep Space / Almost is deliberately quieter than Commons / Now: no fresh feed, no simulated certainty. Just stable constants, explicit approximations, and the strange fact that mystery can be measured.
